# P38: P31 live projection bounded refresh schedule

Status: additive schedule only, **no Standard/Deep Graph routing activation**.

The P30 transport rejects a snapshot older than four hours. A daily-only refresh would fail most of the day, so P38 uses `0,5 */2 * * *` in Supabase PostgreSQL UTC. The job runs at minute 0 and 5 of every even UTC hour. The second invocation provides a retry opportunity without disabling P31's atomic failure behavior. If both attempts fail, the last good snapshot remains committed but becomes unreadable under P30 freshness after four hours and under row-level security after the UTC date changes.

The only SQL command scheduled is `select public.reference_graph_refresh_profile_runtime_projection_p31();`. Jobs execute as the existing privileged `postgres` cron owner. P31 validates the full P7 source and performs a transactional replace of the versioned projection. No publishable credentials, outbound HTTP, additional grants, Quick changes or router changes are introduced.

**Operator acceptance**: verify one job exists with the exact name/schedule/command, `active = true`, that cron executions succeed in `cron.job_run_details`, and that P31 counts and source membership hash match P7. Verify RLS and role grants as before. A successfully registered cron schedule is not evidence that future jobs have already run; monitor upcoming job runs and freshness. Undo only with `select cron.unschedule('reference-graph-p31-profile-refresh');` under an explicitly authorized rollback. Do not touch the existing P10 job.

Standard/Deep activation remains default-off. Offline and live 48-case parity evidence must be separately assessed for each new snapshot.

**Monitoring:** P39 adds a read-only, fixture-testable health evaluator and alert planner. It is not scheduled or activated by this change. Thresholds, snapshot shape and the runbook are documented in `P39.md`.
