# Copy authenticity

Visible words are part of product quality. Review them for truth, specificity,
brand voice, and repeated model-generated patterns.

## Non-negotiable truth rules

- Do not invent metrics, testimonials, customer logos, people, companies, awards, or product claims and present them as factual.
- Do not present Lorem Ipsum, placeholder identities, or fabricated evidence as shippable product truth.
- Error copy must give enough information for recovery when the product can provide it.
- If sample/demo data is intentionally required, label it as sample or otherwise keep its non-factual status clear in the product context.

## Contextual copy tells

These are review leads, not universal bans. The selected brand voice or explicit
user copy may intentionally use them.

- Repetitive em/en-dash cadence or other punctuation tics.
- Empty marketing verbs such as Elevate, Seamless, Unleash, Supercharge,
  Revolutionize, Empower, or Streamline when they carry no concrete meaning.
- Generic placeholder-style names such as Acme, Nexus, SmartFlow, John Doe, or Jane Doe.
- Emoji in UI chrome when no playful/product voice supports it.
- Title Case On Every Label without a system reason.
- Mock-humble craftsman prose or formulaic constructions such as "not just X, but Y" when they do not match the product voice.

Project copy guidelines and supplied factual copy have authority over generic
style heuristics. Do not rewrite exact required copy merely to satisfy a taste
preference.

## Prefer

- Action labels that name the action, such as "Save changes", when a generic label would be ambiguous.
- Specific claims over abstract vibes.
- Sentence case when it matches the product system.
- Error text that explains the recovery step.
- One stable label for the same recurring action unless context requires a different one.

## Length and shape heuristics

Shorter marketing headlines and supporting copy often scan better, but exact word
counts are not a gate. Fit copy to hierarchy, viewport, localization risk, and the
actual product message. Real quotes can be longer when the layout and evidence
support them.

## Human review

Before final verification, re-read visible strings and check:

- unclear referents;
- broken parallel structure;
- unsupported claims;
- repeated formulaic cadence;
- copy that sounds unlike the selected product voice;
- labels that hide what the action will do.

Plain and true beats clever and false.

## Metrics policy

| Evidence state | Action |
|---|---|
| Real, verified data is available | Use it according to product/source policy |
| Mock/sample data is explicitly required | Use clearly non-factual sample data appropriate to the environment |
| No data exists but layout expects stats | Change the layout or mark a non-shipping placeholder clearly |
| Social proof is unavailable | Omit it rather than inventing it |

A stat-led production surface without real authorised numbers is HOLD under the
truth gates in `anti-slop-gates.md`.
