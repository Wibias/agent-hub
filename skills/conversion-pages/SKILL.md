# Conversion Pages: Landing + Pricing

Design pages that convert. One offer, one audience, one primary action.

## Which page type?
- **Landing page**: single-offer, campaign-specific, one CTA. See `references/landing-page.md`.
- **Pricing page**: plan selection, uncertainty reduction, upgrade triggers. See `references/pricing-page.md`.

Read the matching reference file for page-specific structure, layout types, and templates.

## Shared CRO Principles

### 1. Clarity before cleverness
- State the offer in plain language above the fold.
- Benefit-first copy: what the user gets, not what the product does.
- Use specificity: "Cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes", not "Save time".

### 2. One primary action per page
- One CTA pattern per page intent. No competing verbs ("Get started" + "Learn more" + "Try free" in the same hero).
- Make the next step visually obvious. Size, contrast, and position should make it impossible to miss.

### 3. Match message to traffic source
- Ads: mirror the ad headline in the hero.
- Search: match the keyword intent in H1 and title tag.
- Email: assume the reader is warm; skip overexplaining the product.

### 4. Reduce risk at every objection point
Offer at least one:
- Free trial / free plan
- Cancel anytime
- Money-back guarantee
- No credit card required

### 5. Proof belongs near claims
- Do not collect all proof into one testimonials block at the bottom.
- A stat or logo sits next to the claim it supports.
- Testimonials work best placed near the decision point (just before or after the CTA).

### 6. FAQ is objection handling, not an afterthought
- Place FAQ earlier for high-friction offers (enterprise, high price, complex product).
- Write each Q/A to resolve a specific conversion-blocking doubt.

## AIDA Structure (universal scaffold)
1. **Attention** -- Headline + hero visual: what it is, who it's for, why now.
2. **Interest** -- Problem/solution + benefits: why the user should care.
3. **Desire** -- Proof + social signals: why others trust it.
4. **Action** -- CTA + risk reversal: what to do now, why it's safe.

## Copywriting Patterns

### Headline formulas
- "{Outcome} without {pain}"
- "The {category} for {audience}"
- "{Result} in {time frame}"
- "Plans that scale from {small} to {big}"

### CTA rules
- Verb + what they get: "Start free trial", "Book a demo", "Get the checklist".
- Avoid: "Learn more", "Submit", "Click here".
- One CTA intent per hero; repeat it verbatim at the bottom.

### Benefit bullets
Format: **Benefit** -- proof/detail.
Example: **Faster iteration** -- generate 3 layout variants in one click.

### Plan description (2 lines for pricing)
- Line 1: who it's for.
- Line 2: what it unlocks.

## SEO/AEO Checklist (both page types)
- `noindex` for ad-only campaigns; index evergreen offers.
- Title: keyword-first, 50-60 chars, includes the offer.
- Meta: value prop + call-to-action in 150-160 chars.
- FAQ written in plain Q/A format for answer-engine visibility.
- Internal links from homepage and feature pages to this URL.

---

## Output Format (when generating a page)
1. Page outline (sections + order)
2. Hero copy (headline, subheadline, CTA, proof line)
3. Benefits (3-5 bullets)
4. FAQ (6-12 Q/A)
5. SEO/AEO (title, meta, indexing recommendation)
6. Layout recommendation (with rationale)

---

## Quick Diagnostic Questions
- What is the ONE primary CTA?
- Who is the ICP and what is the main pain?
- What risk reversal is available?
- Where is the traffic coming from?
- What proof exists (logos, testimonials, numbers)?
- For pricing: what is the value metric? Which plan is recommended?
